<span style="color:null;font-family:'Arial';"><h1 id="cybercriminals_and_you"><center><u>Cybercriminals and You</u></center></h1><hr>
<p>This book will help your machines and, by extension, you survive many assaults the invisible world has to offer.</p><hr>
  <p><h2><b>Syndira</b></h2>
  Most likely the most dangerous of them all, this Syndicate-sponsored
  threat group has the goal of destroying every single ThinkDos-capable computer on your station.
  They are fond of the Grifeload malware strain, which they equip with the capability to spread
  instantly and everywhere, albeit with a delay.</p>
<p><h2><b>3x80-Tide</b></h2>
Also known as "GreyTide", this group aims for the station Artificial Intelligence. Any vulnerability
in the AI may result in rogue laws, but it will take several, if not many, attempts to reach corruption.
Despite that, 3x80-Tide remains a critical threat and it is up to the crew to recognize it.
Their malware-of-choice is rather unremarkable, if you ignore its AI-seeking capabilities.
<p><h2><b>PLASMASTEEL</b></h2>
The power eaters. While their goal is simply money, their mining malware is incredibly inefficient,
resulting in major power drain. Their go-to is ElectrumXX with the addition of continous-spreading
capability, known as "clock-worm".
<p><h2><b>The Cluwne Company</b></h2>
While this group's malware does not cause direct harm, the mass-spread inconvenience might do it instead.
They are infamous for the common CluwneBash malware strain, which locks the computer's screen with
an image of a clown, necessitating a reboot. Now, that in itself wouldn't earn them the APT title.
It is the persistence and mass-spread capability of their "proprietary" malware that causes concern.
